How this room works

01

Bring yourself, not your LinkedIn version.

Bring the win, the pitch, the doubt, the rant, and the dilemma.

02

Share like a person, not like a campaign.

Share what you are building. Ask for help and feedback. Pitch when it makes sense.

03

Keep it in the room, treat it with care.

Privacy is the baseline here. Do not carry someone’s context outside the group.

04

The point is to help, not to sound smart.

Share the advice. Make the intro. Drop the resource. Be direct. Be generous.
